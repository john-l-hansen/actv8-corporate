We enable the connection of media to mobile commerce while adding value throughout the user's world journey. We do it in four ways:

TV

Inaudible audio triggers enable the instant transfer of offers from the TV to the consumer’s mobile device.

Radio

Delivers synchronized content, empowering artists and brands to create a deeper relationship with fans.

Retail

Driving purchase intent, brand awareness and sales in the retail environment.

In-App

Our Mobii SDK was built for streamlined integration into existing mobile applications.

TV

The fully robust platform enables viewers the ability to synchronously shop while watching in real-time or time shifted and receive personalized offers.The dashboard can hyper-target viewers based on their age, gender, location and media consumption behavior, enabling advertisers to connect and communicate with their target audience across all screens at the same time.

Radio

During radio broadcasts, music streaming or commercials, audio can deliver offers directly to consumer’s mobile devices, which can then be saved and instantly redeemed online or in-Store. Ultimately, creating an ancillary distribution channel, and empowering artists and brands to create a deeper relationship with consumers.

Retail

Our technology provides an enhanced retail experience for shoppers by leveraging our bluetooth beacons, audio recognition capabilities and geolocation. Consumers are targeted all along the path to purchase with a contextual mobile wallet that delivers value to inspire transaction, brand awareness and loyalty.

In-App

Mobii is a free mobile application that delivers offers and coupons from the world around you, which can be instantly redeemed online or in-store. Within Mobii, users receive 10 Daily Scratchers, giving them the chance to win exclusive offers, prizes and more. Brands receive prime real estate within the Daily Scratchers rotation, allowing them to drive brand awareness, sales and deliver value in a way never before done. The platform has an available Software Development Kit, built for streamlined integration into applications with existing user bases. The SDK provides features such as: offer detection, mobile wallet functionality and our daily scratchers component.

How we do it.

Through proprietary inaudible audio triggers embedded in content, geo-fencing technology and Bluetooth beacons.

Audio Triggers

Inaudible audio triggers enable the instant transfer of offers and prizes from any major media channel.

Bluetooth Beacons

Beacon capability allows users to interact with tagged items within close proximity, sending information and value instantaneously.

Geo-Fencing

Send targeted incentives to users based on location, maximizing engagement and conversions.
